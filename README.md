# markdown-level-3
## command line
- command mkdir creates the folder
---
- this markdown creates the subheading/subtitle
for excample:
## cat 
---
- *text* the symbol makes the word italics 
for example: 
*tell stories*
---
- **text**-bolds the text words
for excample: 
**milk**
---
- the backtick symbol will create a single line of code (')
---
- the triple backtick symbol will create a block of code or multiple lines of code (''')
---
- The dash symbol creates a bullet list/unordered list 
for excample = -pizza
               -coke
               -rice
---
- The number with a . and a space will create a numbered list 
for excample = 1. ela
               2. math
               3. biology
               4. history
---
- the format of a link is [text](link)
-  for excample [dog](https://www.countryliving.com/life/kids-pets/g3283/the-50-most-popular-dog-breeds/)
  ---
-  the link to an image is ![dog](https://www.countryliving.com/life/kids-pets/g3283/the-50-most-popular-dog-breeds/)
  ---
 -  The triple dashes creates the horizontal link breaks (---)
  for excample:
  - cat
  ---
  - dogs
  ---
  - rabbits
  ---
